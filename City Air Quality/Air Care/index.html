📊 About Air Quality Index

Air Quality Index (AQI) indicates the level of air pollution in an area. Higher values indicate greater pollution and potential health concerns.

0–50: Good

51–100: Satisfactory

101–200: Moderate

201–300: Poor

301–400: Very Poor

401–500: Severe

Note: The displayed AQI is an indicative PM2.5-based estimate, not an official CPCB station reading. Real-world monitoring values can differ.
